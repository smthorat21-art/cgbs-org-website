📚 पुस्तके व शैक्षणिक साहित्य

Books, notebooks, school bags, stationery and competitive exam books.

🧸 खेळणी व शैक्षणिक साहित्य

Toys, puzzles, educational games and learning aids.

💻 डिजिटल उपकरणे

Working laptops, computers, mobiles and tablets, subject to inspection.

🪑 फर्निचर व घरगुती उपयोगाच्या वस्तू

Study tables, chairs, bookshelves, cupboards, beds and office furniture.

🦽 वैद्यकीय व सहाय्यक उपकरणे

Wheelchairs, walkers, commode chairs, walking sticks and functional nebulizers.

⚽ क्रीडा साहित्य

Sports kits, shoes and usable sports equipment.

🎁 इतर उपयुक्त वस्तू

Other useful items based on verified community needs.

03. तपासणी व वर्गीकरण
