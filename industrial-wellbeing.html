CGBS aims to promote workplace safety awareness and employee wellbeing through modern Virtual Reality (VR) technology-based workshops and awareness programmes.

Experience Learning Through VR

Virtual Reality can help participants experience simulated workplace situations in an immersive learning environment. Workshops can be designed according to the needs of industries and participating organisations.

1. VR-Based Industrial Safety Awareness

Interactive awareness sessions using suitable VR simulations to help workers understand workplace hazards, safe practices and accident prevention.

2. Employee Mental Wellbeing

Awareness workshops on stress management, emotional wellbeing, healthy work habits and the importance of a supportive workplace.

3. Healthy Workforce Awareness

Programmes promoting health awareness, personal wellbeing, healthy habits and a positive approach to work and life.

4. Sustainable Workplace Awareness

Awareness sessions encouraging responsible workplace practices, a positive safety culture and a healthier industrial environment.
