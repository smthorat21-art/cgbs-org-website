CGBS Youth Support Programme

Supporting Potential • Creating Opportunities • Building Futures

A platform to connect young people with appropriate support, opportunities and resources to pursue their goals and potential.

About the Programme

CGBS Youth Support Programme is designed to identify and present documented support opportunities for young people in education, sports, arts, research, handicrafts and entrepreneurship. Each case can be reviewed on the basis of available information and supporting documents before being presented for support.

Transparent & Document-Based Approach

CGBS will present support opportunities based on available information, supporting documents, consent and case-specific verification. Personal and sensitive information should not be published publicly unless appropriate consent is available.
