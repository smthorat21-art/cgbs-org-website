MOM – One Story, Yours & Mine

Family Communication Workshop

Creating space for listening, understanding, communication and positive family engagement.

About MOM

MOM – One Story, Yours & Mine is a family-focused awareness and communication workshop designed to encourage meaningful conversations between children, parents and families.

Why Family Communication Matters

Every family has its own experiences, challenges and stories. Positive communication can help family members listen to one another, understand different perspectives and build supportive relationships.

Our Approach

👂 Listening

Encouraging children and family members to listen with patience and attention.

💬 Communication

Promoting open, respectful and age-appropriate conversations within families.

🤝 Understanding

Helping family members recognise different feelings, experiences and perspectives.

❤️ Positive Engagement

Encouraging supportive family interaction and constructive participation.

For Schools, Families & Communities

The workshop can be conducted with schools, families, youth groups and community organisations as an awareness and engagement activity.

Workshop Enrolment

Interested schools, families, youth groups or community organisations can contact CGBS for workshop information and enrolment.
