About MOM

MOM is a family communication initiative designed to encourage meaningful dialogue, understanding and positive engagement between children, parents and families.

Why Family Communication Matters

Open communication can help children feel heard and supported while helping parents better understand their children’s experiences, concerns and aspirations.
