Our Purpose

CGBS believes that meaningful social progress is not only about providing immediate support.

It is about creating the right opportunities, skills, knowledge, resources and encouragement so that people can move forward with confidence and independence.

Our aim is not simply to give support, but to create opportunities for people to become capable, confident, independent and self-reliant.
