About CGBS

Chhatrapati Gramvikas Bhauuddhashiya Sanstha (CGBS) works towards social progress and better life by supporting education, empowerment, health, skills, livelihood and community development.

We believe meaningful social development happens when people receive the right opportunities, knowledge, skills, resources and encouragement.

Our approach is to empower people — not create dependency.
