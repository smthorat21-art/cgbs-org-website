About CGBS

Chhatrapati Gramvikas Bahuuddeshiya Sanstha (CGBS) works towards social progress and a better life by supporting education, empowerment, health, skills, livelihood and community development.

Our purpose is not simply to give support, but to create opportunities that help people become capable, confident, independent and self-reliant.

We believe meaningful social development happens when people are given the right opportunities, knowledge, resources, skills and encouragement.
