About AIM

AIM focuses on awareness, guidance and positive engagement to help young people and families understand addiction-related risks and seek constructive support.

Building Awareness • Strengthening Support • Encouraging Positive Choices

The workshop approach is designed for youth, families, schools and communities, with emphasis on communication, guidance and healthy engagement.
