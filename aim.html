AIM – Youth De-Addiction Workshop

Awareness, guidance and positive engagement for young people and families.

About AIM

A community-focused workshop designed to create awareness, encourage healthy choices and help young people and families understand the importance of a supportive, positive environment.

Our Approach

The workshop focuses on awareness, communication, guidance and constructive engagement. It aims to create a safe space where young people and families can learn, discuss concerns and explore positive pathways.

🌱 Awareness

Understanding risks and building awareness among young people and families.

🤝 Guidance

Encouraging supportive communication and responsible guidance.

💬 Family Support

Strengthening family communication and positive engagement.

🎯 Positive Engagement

Promoting healthy activities, constructive participation and future-focused thinking.

Workshop Enrolment

Interested schools, families, youth groups or community organisations can contact CGBS for workshop information and enrolment.
